Name origin note for designers:
  Latin "tessera" = a mosaic tile, AND a Roman token/ticket (tessera
  frumentaria = a grain-dole ticket), AND a gaming die. The three meanings
  map exactly to: a pool of many pieces, case = real ticket, and the draw.
